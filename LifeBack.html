2 Ilova nima qiladi

LifeBack — foydalanuvchi tanlagan ilovalarda (masalan, ijtimoiy tarmoqlar, video ilovalar, o'yinlar) qancha vaqt o'tkazilganini kuzatib, belgilangan vaqt chegarasiga yetganda ekranda motivatsion eslatma ko'rsatuvchi ilova. Ilova foydalanuvchiga o'z maqsadlarini ("goals") belgilash, ularni eslab qolish va kunlik bajarish ketma-ketligini (streak) kuzatish imkonini beradi.

1 Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what information the "LifeBack" mobile application (the "App") has access to, how it is used, and how it is protected. By using the App, you agree to the terms of this policy.

Developer: Zetra Studio
Contact: biznesmusoxon@gmail.com

2 What the App does

LifeBack tracks how long you've spent in apps you choose to monitor (e.g. social media, video, or game apps) and shows a motivational full-screen reminder once a time threshold you set is reached. The App also lets you set personal "goals," track them, and keep a daily completion streak.

3 Information we collect

Important: LifeBack never sends your personal usage data (which apps you use and for how long, your goals, your settings) to any server. All such data is stored locally on your device only.

Data Type Google Play purchase data (subscriptions, lifetime)

LifeBack never reads on-screen text, messages, passwords, or any other personal content. The Accessibility Service is used solely to detect which app is open and for how long (canRetrieveWindowContent="false").

4 Advertising

LifeBack does not use any third-party ad network (not Google AdMob, not any other ad network). The ad banner shown in the free version is simple text-and-image content fully managed by the developer and fetched from the developer's own hosted file.

No personal data (device identifiers, location, advertising ID, etc.) is ever sent or collected through this ad system. The App simply makes a plain network request (HTTP GET) to load ad text/images.

5 Permissions

About the Accessibility Service: LifeBack uses it only to detect which app is open and for how long. It does not read screen content, messages, passwords or anything you type. You are asked for explicit consent inside the app before being sent to Android settings. If "Return to home screen" is on, the service presses the Home button after you dismiss a reminder; you can turn this off in Settings. The data never leaves your device.

6 Third-party services

The App uses the following third-party service:

  • Google Play Billing — used for subscriptions, the one-time lifetime purchase, and optional donations. Payment information is handled by Google Play. See: Google Privacy Policy.

The App does not use Google AdMob or any other ad network — so no advertising identifier or similar tracking data is collected.

7 Data retention and deletion

All personal settings, goals, and usage statistics are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the App permanently deletes all associated data. Since we do not operate a central server storing your data, there is no need to submit a data-deletion request to us — uninstalling the App achieves the same result.

8 Children's privacy

LifeBack is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used this App, please contact us.

9 Security

All personal usage data is stored in your device's protected internal storage (Android SharedPreferences), inaccessible to other apps. Because this data is never transmitted to an external server, there is no risk of data interception over the network.
