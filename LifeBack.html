2. Ilova nima qiladi

LifeBack — foydalanuvchi tanlagan ilovalarda (masalan, ijtimoiy tarmoqlar, video ilovalar, o'yinlar) qancha vaqt o'tkazilganini kuzatib, belgilangan vaqt chegarasiga yetganda ekranda motivatsion eslatma ko'rsatuvchi ilova. Ilova foydalanuvchiga o'z maqsadlarini ("goals") belgilash, ularni eslab qolish va kunlik bajarish ketma-ketligini (streak) kuzatish imkonini beradi.

4. Reklama tizimi

LifeBack hech qanday tashqi reklama tarmog'idan (Google AdMob yoki boshqa uchinchi tomon reklama tarmog'idan) foydalanmaydi. Bepul versiyada ko'rinadigan reklama banneri to'liq ishlab chiquvchining o'zi tomonidan boshqariladigan, oddiy matn va rasmdan iborat statik kontent bo'lib, u ishlab chiquvchining o'z serveridan (GitHub orqali joylashtirilgan oddiy fayl) yuklab olinadi.

Bu reklama tizimi orqali sizning shaxsiy ma'lumotlaringiz (qurilma identifikatori, joylashuv, reklama identifikatori va h.k.) hech qayerga yuborilmaydi va yig'ilmaydi — ilova shunchaki reklama rasmi/matnini ko'rsatish uchun oddiy tarmoq so'rovi (HTTP GET) yuboradi, xuddi veb-sahifa rasm yuklaganidek. Reklama bosilganda, brauzeringiz orqali tegishli havola ochiladi.

7. Ma'lumotlarni saqlash va o'chirish

Barcha shaxsiy sozlamalar, maqsadlar va foydalanish statistikasi faqat qurilmangizda saqlanadi. Ilovani qurilmangizdan o'chirsangiz, barcha ma'lumot butunlay yo'q qilinadi. Bizda sizning ma'lumotlaringizni saqlaydigan markaziy server yo'q, shuning uchun "ma'lumotlarimni o'chirib bering" so'rovini yuborishga hojat yo'q — buni ilovani o'chirish orqali o'zingiz amalga oshira olasiz.

8. Bolalar maxfiyligi

LifeBack 13 yoshdan kichik bolalarga qaratilgan emas va ulardan ongli ravishda ma'lumot to'plamaydi. Agar 13 yoshdan kichik bolangiz ushbu ilovadan foydalanganini bilib qolsangiz, biz bilan bog'laning.

9. Xavfsizlik

Barcha shaxsiy foydalanish ma'lumoti qurilmangizning ichki xotirasida, boshqa ilovalar kira olmaydigan himoyalangan joyda (Android SharedPreferences) saqlanadi. Bu ma'lumot hech qachon tashqi serverga yuborilmagani uchun, tarmoq orqali sizib chiqish xavfi mavjud emas.

2. What the App does

LifeBack tracks how long you've spent in apps you choose to monitor (e.g. social media, video, or game apps) and shows a motivational full-screen reminder once a time threshold you set is reached. The App also lets you set personal "goals," track them, and keep a daily completion streak.

3. Information we collect

Important: LifeBack never sends your personal usage data (which apps you use and for how long, your goals, your settings) to any server. All such data is stored locally on your device only.

LifeBack never reads on-screen text, messages, passwords, or any other personal content. The Accessibility Service is used solely to detect which app is open and for how long (canRetrieveWindowContent="false" — the ability to read screen content is technically disabled).

4. Advertising

LifeBack does not use any third-party ad network (not Google AdMob, not any other ad network). The ad banner shown in the free version is simple text-and-image content fully managed by the developer and fetched from the developer's own hosted file (via GitHub).

No personal data (device identifiers, location, advertising ID, etc.) is ever sent or collected through this ad system — the App simply makes a plain network request (HTTP GET) to load ad text/images, similar to a web page loading an image. Tapping the ad opens the relevant link in your browser.

7. Data retention and deletion

All personal settings, goals, and usage statistics are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the App permanently deletes all associated data. Since we do not operate a central server storing your data, there is no need to submit a data-deletion request to us — uninstalling the App achieves the same result.

8. Children's privacy

LifeBack is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used this App, please contact us.

9. Security

All personal usage data is stored in your device's protected internal storage (Android SharedPreferences), inaccessible to other apps. Because this data is never transmitted to an external server, there is no risk of data interception over the network.
