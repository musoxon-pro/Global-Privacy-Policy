Cometra does not collect, sell or share your personal information. We do not run a server that receives your gameplay.

1. What stays on your device

To remember where you left off, the game saves the following only on your phone:

  • High score, Stardust (Chang) and Zetra balances
  • Upgrade levels, owned and selected skins
  • Settings: sound mode, music and effects volume, vibration, break reminder
  • The date of your last Daily Gift, and the app’s sky style (so it can change next time)
  • Identifiers of completed Google Play purchases, so one purchase is never credited twice

This data is not linked to your name, email or any account, and it is never sent to us. Auto-backup is turned off for Cometra, so it is not copied to the cloud by Android either.

2. Purchases

Optional purchases (Zetra, Stardust packs and support) are handled entirely by Google Play Billing. Google processes your payment under its own terms and Privacy Policy. We never see your card number, billing address or Google account details.

After a successful payment, Google tells the app on your device which product you bought, and the app adds it to your balance.

3. Permissions

PermissionWhy
VibrationGentle haptic feedback when comets are destroyed. You can switch it off in Settings.

Cometra never asks for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

4. Ads and third parties

Cometra is 100% ad-free. It contains no advertising networks, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and no social logins. The only third-party service it uses is Google Play (for installing the app and for purchases).

If you have allowed it in your Android settings, Google may collect anonymous diagnostics (for example crashes) for apps installed from Google Play. That is controlled by Google and your device, not by us.

5. Children

Cometra is intended for ages 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Since the game collects nothing, there is nothing to remove, but if you believe a child has contacted us, write to us and we will delete it right away.

6. Your choices and deleting your data

  • To erase everything the game saved: open Android Settings → Apps → Cometra → Storage → Clear data, or simply uninstall the app.
  • Deleting it also removes your progress and in-game balances. Purchases remain connected to your Google account, and Google can help with refunds under its policy.
  • Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export or correct. You are still welcome to ask us anything.

7. Changes to this policy

If the game ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change goes live. Material changes will also be mentioned in the app’s update notes.

Cometra shaxsiy maʼlumotlaringizni yigʻmaydi, sotmaydi va ulashmaydi. Oʻyiningiz maʼlumotini qabul qiladigan serverimiz yoʻq.

1. Qurilmangizda nimalar saqlanadi

Qayerda toʻxtaganingizni eslab qolish uchun oʻyin quyidagilarni faqat telefoningizda saqlaydi:

  • Rekord ball, Chang va Zetra balanslari
  • Upgrade darajalari, sotib olingan va tanlangan skinlar
  • Sozlamalar: ovoz rejimi, musiqa va effektlar balandligi, titrash, tanaffus eslatmasi
  • Oxirgi kunlik sovgʻa sanasi va ilova osmon uslubi (keyingi safar almashishi uchun)

Bu maʼlumot ismingiz, emailingiz yoki biror akkaunt bilan bogʻlanmaydi va bizga yuborilmaydi. Cometra uchun avto-zaxira oʻchirilgan, ya’ni Android ham uni bulutga nusxalamaydi.

2. Xaridlar

Ixtiyoriy xaridlar (Zetra, Chang paketlari va qoʻllab-quvvatlash) toʻliq Google Play Billing orqali amalga oshadi. Toʻlovni Google oʻz shartlari va Maxfiylik siyosati asosida qayta ishlaydi. Biz karta raqamingiz, hisob-kitob manzilingiz yoki Google akkauntingiz maʼlumotlarini koʻrmaymiz.

Toʻlov muvaffaqiyatli oʻtgach, Google qurilmangizdagi ilovaga qaysi mahsulot olinganini bildiradi va ilova uni balansingizga qoʻshadi.
