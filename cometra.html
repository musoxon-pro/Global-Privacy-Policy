Cometra Privacy Policy

The Mission Briefing

Effective 2026-11-11 · Developer: Zetra

TL;DR: No ads. No trackers. No servers of ours holding your data. Your progress lives on your device — and, only if you choose to sign in to Google Play Games, in Google's cloud.
🛰️

1What this is

Cometra is a calm space arcade. A game should relax you, not study you. This page explains in plain words what happens to your data while you smash number comets.
🧑‍🚀

2What stays on your ship

Everything you earn is stored locally on your device: Chang, Zetra, upgrades, skins, ships, your galaxy, high scores and settings (sound, haptics). We never receive it.
☁️

3Optional: Google Play Games

Signing in is optional. If you do, Google handles:
  • Saved Games — your progress is saved in your Google account to restore on a new phone. We never see or access it.
  • Leaderboard 🏆 — your best score is sent to the Google Play Games leaderboard and shown to other players with your Play Games player name and profile picture. You can hide or delete entries in Play Games settings.
This runs on Google's infrastructure under Google's Privacy Policy. Don't sign in → nothing is sent.
🛡️

4What we do NOT do

  • No analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No ads or ad networks
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or sensors
  • No accounts or servers of our own
  • No selling or sharing of your data — we don't have it
The only special permission is vibration (haptics), which you can turn off.
💎

5Treasure shop

Zetra, Chang packs and donations are processed by Google Play Billing. We never see your card, bank details or address.
💾

6Backups

If Android backup is on, your phone may back up progress to your Google Drive and restore it later. Android and Google handle this, not us.
👶

7Young pilots

Cometra is for ages 13+. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone, children included.
🗑️

8Wipe your trail

  • Local data: uninstall the game or clear its storage.
  • Saved Games / leaderboard: Play Games app → Settings.
We hold no data about you, so nothing on our side needs deleting — but write to us if you need help.
🔭

9Changes

If the map changes (new features, new galaxies!), we update this page and the date above.
📡

10Contact Mission Control

Questions? zetra.dev@gmail.com — a real human replies.
Fly safe, Pilot. Your data stays yours. ✨

Cometra maxfiylik siyosati

Missiya yo'riqnomasi

Kuchga kirgan sana: 2026-11-11 · Dasturchi: Zetra

Qisqasi: Reklama yo'q. Kuzatuvchilar yo'q. Ma'lumotingizni saqlaydigan o'z serverimiz yo'q. Progressingiz qurilmangizda turadi — faqat o'zingiz Google Play Games'ga kirsangiz, Google bulutida ham.
🛰️

1Bu nima

Cometra — sokin kosmik arkada. O'yin sizni charchatmasligi kerak, o'rganmasligi kerak. Bu sahifada raqamli kometalarni urayotganingizda ma'lumotlaringiz bilan nima bo'lishi oddiy tilda tushuntiriladi.
🧑‍🚀

2Kemangizda (qurilmangizda) nima qoladi

Yutganlaringiz hammasi qurilmangizda saqlanadi: Chang, Zetra, upgradelar, skinlar, kemalar, galaktika, rekordlar va sozlamalar (ovoz, tebranish). Biz buni hech qachon olmaymiz.
☁️

3Ixtiyoriy: Google Play Games

Kirish ixtiyoriy. Kirsangiz, Google quyidagilarni boshqaradi:
  • Saved Games — progressingiz sizning Google akkauntingizda saqlanadi, yangi telefonda tiklash uchun. Biz uni ko'rmaymiz ham, ochmaymiz ham.
  • Reyting 🏆 — eng yaxshi ballingiz Google Play Games reytingiga yuboriladi va boshqa o'yinchilarga Play Games'dagi ismingiz va profil rasmingiz bilan ko'rinadi. Yozuvlarni Play Games sozlamalarida yashirish yoki o'chirish mumkin.
Bu Google infratuzilmasida, Google maxfiylik siyosati asosida ishlaydi. Kirmasangiz — hech narsa yuborilmaydi.
🛡️

4Biz NIMA QILMAYMIZ

  • Analitika yoki kuzatuv SDK'lari yo'q
  • Reklama va reklama tarmoqlari yo'q
  • Joylashuv, kontaktlar, kamera, mikrofon, sensorlar yo'q
  • O'z akkaunt va serverlarimiz yo'q
  • Ma'lumotingizni sotmaymiz va ulashmaymiz — bizda u yo'q
Yagona maxsus ruxsat — tebranish (haptika), uni o'chirib qo'yish mumkin.
💎

5Xazina do'koni

Zetra, Chang paketlari va xayriyalar Google Play Billing orqali to'lanadi. Karta, bank ma'lumoti yoki manzilingizni biz ko'rmaymiz.
💾

6Zaxira nusxa

Android zaxira yoqilgan bo'lsa, telefon progressni sizning Google Drive'ingizga saqlashi mumkin. Buni Android va Google qiladi, biz emas.
👶

7Yosh uchuvchilar

Cometra 13+ yoshdagilar uchun. Biz hech kimdan, shu jumladan bolalardan ham, ongli ravishda ma'lumot yig'maymiz.
🗑️

8Izingizni o'chiring

  • Mahalliy ma'lumot: o'yinni o'chiring yoki xotirasini tozalang.
  • Saved Games / reyting: Play Games ilovasi → Sozlamalar.
Bizda siz haqingizda ma'lumot yo'q, shuning uchun bizda o'chiriladigan narsa ham yo'q — lekin yordam kerak bo'lsa yozing.
🔭

9O'zgarishlar

Xarita o'zgarsa (yangi imkoniyat, yangi galaktika!), shu sahifa va yuqoridagi sanani yangilaymiz.
📡

10Missiya markazi bilan aloqa
