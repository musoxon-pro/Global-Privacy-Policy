📌 Qisqacha ma'lumot

LifeBack ilovasi markaziy serverda hech qanday shaxsiy ma'lumot saqlamaydi. Sizning barcha ma'lumotlaringiz (maqsadlar, sozlamalar, foydalanish statistikasi) faqat sizning qurilmangizda saqlanadi. Shu sababli, ma'lumotlaringizni o'chirish juda oson — bir necha soniya ichida.

1 Ma'lumotlaringizni qanday o'chirish mumkin

Quyidagi qadamlarni bajaring:

  1. Telefoningizda Sozlamalar (Settings) ilovasini oching.
  2. Ilovalar (Apps) yoki Ilovalarni boshqarish (Manage apps) bo'limiga o'ting.
  3. Ro'yxatdan LifeBack ilovasini toping va ustiga bosing.
  4. "O'chirish" (Uninstall) tugmasini bosing va tasdiqlang.
  5. Tamom! Ilova va u bilan birga barcha shaxsiy ma'lumotlaringiz darhol va butunlay o'chiriladi.

📌 Overview

LifeBack does not store any personal data on a central server. All of your data (goals, settings, usage statistics) is stored only on your own device. This makes deleting your data simple — it takes just a few seconds.

1 How to delete your data

Follow these steps:

  1. Open the Settings app on your phone.
  2. Go to Apps or Manage apps.
  3. Find LifeBack in the list and tap it.
  4. Tap "Uninstall" and confirm.
  5. Done! The app and all of your personal data will be permanently deleted immediately.

2 What data gets deleted

Data type Status
Personal goals you have entered Deleted
List of watched apps and time thresholds Deleted
Daily usage statistics and streak history Deleted
Language, sound, vibration, and quiet-hours settings Deleted
Pro version status (local device flag only) Deleted
Purchase history made through Google Play Retained by Google
Note: Google Play purchase history is managed by Google and we cannot access it. To delete it, please contact the Google Play Help Center.

3 Additional questions

If you cannot uninstall the app or have other questions, contact us. We will respond within 30 days and provide any assistance we can.
